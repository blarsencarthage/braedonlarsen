Throughout my time at Carthage, one time that I am very fortunate to have gotten a lot of experience with, is my in getting oral presentations. Many of these presentations were given in classes either as a oral presentation on a topic, or as a lecture given to the class on a topic.

My sophomore year I was taking PHY2300 Modern Physics, one of the final deliverables for this course was a 20 minute in-class lecture delivered on a modern physics topic, not covered in class. We were expected to go out and independently research, comprehend, and prepare this lecture. My particular topic was a conceptual understand of the nuclear physics necessary in order to understand how nuclear fusion works.

This project was significant, as it was my first major independent solo project with the primary goal of teaching a topic to a room full of people. From both preparing and giving this lecture, I was better able to understand the differences between simply knowing a topic and being able to teach it.
