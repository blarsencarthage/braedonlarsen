## What I Did

NASA Techleap Prize Project (Project ongoing at Carthage)

- Extended theoretical models of acoustic fluid dynamics to predict resonant bubble nucleation behavior in microgravity environments
- Developed Python-based computer vision tools using TrackPy and OpenCV to extract quantitative data from experimental video footage of bubble dynamics
- Performed COMSOL Multiphysics simulations of pressure wave propagation in microgravity fluids to validate theoretical derivations
- Designed data acquisition subsystems and refined payload requirements for future sub-orbital flight experiments
