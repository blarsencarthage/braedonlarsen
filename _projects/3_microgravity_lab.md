## What I Did

### Microgravity Ullage Detection (M.U.D.) · Aug 2024 – Present

- Designed and assembled high-precision mechanical and electrical subsystems for sub-orbital payload hardware
- Updated C-based flight control software and deployed to Linux-based payload computers for autonomous operation
- Created technical documentation and CAD FEA's to meet flight provider safety and interface requirements

### Microgravity Propellant Gauging – P.R.O.T.O · Aug 2024 – May 2025

- Refurbished sub-orbital payload post-launch including an extensive refit of the payloads hardware
- Analyzed video data from microgravity time on sub-orbital flight for evidence of ullage movement
- Prepared sub-orbital payload for decommissioning following final sub-orbital payload including evaluation of hardware for potential reuse

### Microgravity Ullage Trapping Technology · May 2025 – Present

- Continued NASA research in-house at Carthage's microgravity lab under Carthage Faculty
- Designed optical and lighting system of sub-orbital payload for integration with the portions of the design
- Further developed data analysis software to handle 3D-environments and correct visual data for optical effects of camera systems
- Simulated ray-tracing models of sub-orbital payload to evaluate refraction effects and characterize behavior of light
